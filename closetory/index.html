隐私政策 / Privacy Policy 用户条款 / Terms of Use

Closetory 采用本地优先设计。衣物照片、衣橱、搭配和日记数据默认保存在你的设备上；天气功能只有在你主动使用时才会请求位置和网络。

Closetory stores wardrobe content on your device. Optional weather requests, system backups, exports, support emails, and website hosting are explained in the Privacy Policy.
